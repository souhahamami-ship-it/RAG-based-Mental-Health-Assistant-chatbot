Stress is a response that can occur when a person feels that the demands of a situation are difficult to manage. Short periods of stress can be a normal part of life, while prolonged or intense stress may affect wellbeing.

People may experience stress through changes in mood, concentration, sleep, energy, or physical tension. Helpful approaches can include breaking large tasks into smaller steps, taking regular breaks, practicing slow breathing, staying physically active, and talking with a trusted person.

There is no single coping strategy that works for everyone. If stress becomes overwhelming, lasts for a long time, or substantially interferes with daily life, seeking support from a qualified professional may be helpful.
