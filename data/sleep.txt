Sleep plays an important role in physical and mental wellbeing. Poor sleep can affect concentration, mood, energy, and the ability to manage everyday stress.

A consistent sleep schedule can support healthy sleep habits. Going to bed and waking up at similar times each day can help establish a regular routine. A relaxing bedtime routine and a comfortable, quiet sleeping environment may also be helpful.

Caffeine, nicotine, and stimulating activities close to bedtime can interfere with sleep for some people. If sleep problems continue or regularly interfere with daytime functioning, discussing them with a healthcare professional can be appropriate.
