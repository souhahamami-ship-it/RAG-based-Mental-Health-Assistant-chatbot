Anxiety is a common emotional response that can occur when a person feels threatened, uncertain, or under pressure. Some anxiety can be a normal response to stressful situations, but persistent or excessive anxiety may interfere with everyday activities.

Anxiety can involve worry, restlessness, difficulty concentrating, irritability, muscle tension, or changes in sleep. People can experience anxiety differently, and these signs do not by themselves establish a diagnosis.

Simple coping strategies may include slow breathing, maintaining a regular routine, physical activity, reducing avoidable sources of stress, and talking with someone trustworthy. If anxiety is persistent, severe, or significantly affects daily functioning, a qualified mental health professional can provide appropriate assessment and support.
