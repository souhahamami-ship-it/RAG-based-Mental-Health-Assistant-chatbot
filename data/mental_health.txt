Mental health is an important part of overall wellbeing. It can affect how people think, feel, behave, handle stress, and interact with others. Mental wellbeing can change over time depending on life circumstances, relationships, sleep, physical health, and other factors.

Taking care of mental wellbeing can include maintaining regular routines, getting enough sleep, staying physically active, spending time with supportive people, and making time for enjoyable or meaningful activities.

When difficult emotions persist or begin to interfere with daily life, talking with a qualified mental health professional can be a useful step. A professional can help a person understand their situation and identify appropriate forms of support.
